The itch

I play in two redraft leagues on Sleeper, and I already check FantasyCalc to see whether a trade is fair. A trade calculator only answers half the question, though. It tells me two packages are worth about the same, not whether my lineup gets better, or which manager has the hole that my bench can fill. I wanted something that looks at the whole league and brings me the trades worth sending.

What it does

Type a Sleeper username, pick a league, and it looks at every team.

  • Trade ideas, ranked, with both sides, how much each lineup improves, the value balance, and a plain-English reason. Fair, Balanced, and Aggressive change how much the other team has to win too.
  • My team: my best lineup, each position against the league median, and the trade chips worth more in a trade than on my bench. I can lock players I won't move, or shop one to see who'd pay for him.
  • The league: every team's strengths and holes in one table. Open a team to target one of its players.
  • An evaluator for building any trade by hand, or tweaking one of the ideas.
  • Links on every player to FantasyCalc, ESPN, FantasyPros, and Sleeper, for the latest news and injuries before I hit send.

How it works

Your browser does almost everything. It pulls the league's rosters and settings straight from Sleeper, and rest-of-season values from FantasyCalc for that exact format: team count, PPR, and superflex. Then it builds each team's best lineup from the league's real starting slots, and tries the one-for-one and two-for-one swaps between teams whose needs fit together.

A trade only makes the list if my lineup gets better and the other manager gets fair value back. Packages are compared the way trade calculators do it, so two decent players don't automatically buy a star.

Sleeper asks apps to download its full player list (about 5 MB) at most once a day. So a small Lambda fetches it at 6 pm New York time, after the Friday injury reports, and saves the few hundred KB the app needs. Values and rosters are always live. Anything newer than that evening's list is one click away in the player links.

What's next

  • A second opinion on value. Blend in Sleeper's projections, scored with each league's own settings, and FantasyPros' expert rankings.
  • Buy low, sell high. Where projections and the trade market disagree, that's the trade to make.
  • Share links that open a specific league, team, and trade.
  • Keeper and dynasty leagues, with draft picks and age.
