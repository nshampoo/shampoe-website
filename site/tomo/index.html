The itch

My college teammates at ASU and I are runners at heart. For years we dreamed of a world where you could make new friends through running: visit a new town and immediately have someone to run and explore with. So we built it.

What it does

  • Profiles built for runners: photos, a short bio, your usual pace range, and how far you like to go.
  • Matching on pace, distance from you, and your preferences, with a swipe deck to say yes or pass.
  • Chat with your matches to plan the run.
  • Run meetups: post a run with a time, place, distance, and pace, and your matches can join.
  • Push notifications for new matches, friend requests, and meetups.

How it works

One React Native codebase built with Expo, backed by Firebase. The app talks to Firebase directly for sign-in and data, and Cloud Functions react to changes, like sending a push when you get a new match.

What's next

Getting more runners on it, city by city. An app like this is only as good as the people near you, so if you run, download it and say hi.
